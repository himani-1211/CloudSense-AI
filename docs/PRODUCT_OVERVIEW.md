# What is CloudSense AI?

CloudSense AI is an intelligent cloud operations platform that sits on top of cloud providers like AWS.

It brings together cloud infrastructure, operational insights, reports, incidents, and AI assistance into a single platform, helping engineers understand their cloud environment faster and make better operational decisions.

CloudSense AI **does not replace cloud providers**.

Instead, it works alongside them by transforming cloud data into meaningful operational intelligence.

---

# The Problem

Modern cloud environments are becoming increasingly complex.

A typical engineer may manage:

- Hundreds of virtual machines
- Multiple databases
- Storage services
- Serverless functions
- Networking resources
- Monitoring tools
- Incident tracking systems

While cloud providers offer excellent management consoles, engineers often need to switch between multiple dashboards just to understand the current state of their infrastructure.

Finding the actual issue often takes longer than fixing it.

---

# How CloudSense AI Helps

CloudSense AI provides a single workspace for cloud operations.

Instead of searching across multiple services, engineers receive a centralized view of their infrastructure along with intelligent insights that help them prioritize what deserves attention.

The platform is designed to reduce operational complexity by organizing cloud information into meaningful modules.

---

# Understanding Each Module

## 📊 Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of the overall cloud environment.

Instead of inspecting every service individually, engineers can immediately understand the overall health of their infrastructure and identify areas that require attention.

**Purpose**

> "What should I look at today?"

---

## 🏗 Infrastructure

Infrastructure acts as the inventory of cloud resources.

It provides visibility into services such as EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, VPC, and EBS, helping engineers understand what resources currently exist.

**Purpose**

> "What resources do I own?"

---

## ⚙ Operations

Operations focuses on what is happening right now.

It provides operational metrics such as resource health, utilization, and system activity, allowing engineers to monitor the current state of their infrastructure.

**Purpose**

> "What is happening in my cloud?"

---

## 🚨 Incidents

Incidents track operational issues that require investigation.

Rather than monitoring raw metrics, engineers can review active incidents, their severity, and their current resolution status.

**Purpose**

> "What problems need immediate attention?"

---

## 📈 Reports

Reports summarize historical cloud activity.

They help teams review infrastructure performance, operational trends, and cloud usage over time.

**Purpose**

> "What happened over the past day, week, or month?"

---

## 🔗 Integrations

Cloud environments rarely consist of a single platform.

The Integrations module is designed to connect cloud providers and operational tools into a unified ecosystem.

Current implementation focuses on AWS, while future versions are designed to support additional cloud providers and DevOps platforms.

**Purpose**

> "Bring all cloud services into one place."

---

## 🤖 AI Copilot

The AI Copilot acts as the intelligent assistant within CloudSense AI.

Rather than replacing the other modules, it uses information from Dashboard, Infrastructure, Operations, Reports, Incidents, and Integrations to answer questions, explain infrastructure behavior, and provide meaningful recommendations.

**Purpose**

> "Help me understand what matters."

---

# Why Not Just Use AWS Console?

AWS provides excellent cloud infrastructure management.

CloudSense AI is designed to complement—not replace—cloud providers.

| AWS Console | CloudSense AI |
|-------------|---------------|
| Shows cloud resources | Explains what matters |
| Displays metrics | Highlights important insights |
| Requires manual investigation | Centralizes operational information |
| Focuses on individual services | Provides a unified operational view |
| Shows cloud data | Helps engineers understand cloud behavior |

CloudSense AI is designed to reduce the time engineers spend searching for information so they can spend more time solving problems.
