Most major cloud providers publish a "well-architected framework" (AWS Well-Architected, Azure
Well-Architected Framework, Google Cloud Architecture Framework). Across all of them, the core
pillars are broadly the same:

- Operational excellence: run and monitor systems to deliver business value, and continually
  improve processes.
- Security: protect data, systems, and assets through risk assessment and mitigation.
- Reliability: ensure a workload performs its intended function correctly and consistently,
  including recovery from failure.
- Performance efficiency: use computing resources efficiently and keep up with evolving demand.
- Cost optimization: avoid unnecessary costs and understand where money is being spent.
- Sustainability: minimize the environmental impact of running workloads.

When evaluating any piece of infrastructure, it helps to ask which of these pillars it might be
weak in, rather than optimizing only for the pillar that's top of mind.
